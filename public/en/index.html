QUESTIONS ABOUT THE TOOL

FAQ

Where do I upload llms.txt+

In the root of the domain, at /llms.txt. On website builders without root access, serve it through a proxy or an edge worker.

Does every site need one+

No. It helps most on sites with documentation, services, or a large content section, where important pages are hard to find by crawling.

Can I list every page+

You can, but a long list dilutes the signal. Keep it to the pages you want cited.

Does the tool publish the file for me+

No. It builds and validates the file. Publishing happens on your side.
